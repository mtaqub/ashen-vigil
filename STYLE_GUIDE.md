# Ashen Vigil: style guide

## Creative direction

The experience should feel ancient, severe, lonely, and ceremonial. Its world is a decaying kingdom sustained by broken oaths, where heroes inherit dangerous relics during a final night watch. The desired reference space is ruined dark fantasy, medieval tragedy, gothic architecture, and apocalyptic folklore—not any specific copyrighted world.

## Visual language

### Palette

- **Void charcoal:** `#120E19` for night, deep panels, and negative space.
- **Funeral slate:** `#342A44` for stone, armor, and secondary surfaces.
- **Bone parchment:** `#F5E7D1` for readable text and rare highlights.
- **Cinder red:** `#DD2C53` for health, danger, and blood shards.
- **Tarnished gold:** `#F6BE4D` for bosses, relics, and important milestones.
- **Blackflame violet:** `#6B2F8B` for forbidden magic.

Keep most of the screen desaturated. Saturated color should signal a gameplay event: damage, a collectible, a relic choice, a boss, or an attack telegraph.

### Materials and silhouettes

- Favor basalt, slate, corroded metal, cracked stone, dead wood, tattered cloth, ash, and restrained embers.
- Build landmarks from large readable silhouettes: leaning grave markers, broken arches, empty thrones, bell towers, chained effigies, and crowned giants.
- Enemies should be recognizable from overhead through shape before texture. Small foes are narrow or winged; tanks are broad; bosses are tall, crowned, and asymmetrical.
- Magic should look ritualistic: circles, sigils, thorns, chains, brands, bells, and smoke. Avoid bright generic sci-fi particles.

## Interface language

- Use charcoal panels, thin tarnished-gold borders, bone-colored type, and ember-red health bars.
- Keep the combat interface minimal. Large ceremonial announcements are reserved for boss arrivals, relic evolutions, and victory.
- Wording should be solemn and concise: **Inherit**, **Endure**, **Oath Sundered**, **Vigil**, **Relic**, **Forsaken**, **Cinder**.
- Do not imitate another game's exact fonts, menu layout, death message, icons, or transition effects.

## Gameplay identity

- **Ashen Dart:** the reliable starting relic.
- **Grave Halo:** controls nearby crowds with expanding ritual rings.
- **Blackflame Testament:** punishes distant clusters with volatile forbidden fire.
- **Cinder Oath:** establishes the build's risk/reward identity by trading health for power.
- **The Cinder Warden:** teaches boss telegraphs through a slow expanding ring before its area strike.

Future relics should have clear tactical jobs rather than being cosmetic reskins: crowd control, focused boss damage, defense, mobility, collection, or risky power.

## Next content set

1. **Bell of the Nameless:** periodic shockwave that briefly slows enemies; evolves into *The Final Toll*.
2. **Thorned Greatblade:** slow frontal cleave with heavy damage; evolves into *Briar Oath*.
3. **Widow's Lantern:** orbiting flame that collects shards and burns nearby foes; evolves into *Moonless Beacon*.
4. **Reliquary Shield:** blocks one hit on a cooldown; evolves into *Sepulcher Aegis*.
5. **Boss—The Weeping Castellan:** a ruined knight that charges along a visible lane.
6. **Boss—Saint of Hollow Bells:** a floating multi-phase enemy that summons expanding sound rings.

## Audio direction

Use original or properly licensed audio only. Favor distant bells, low bowed strings, wind through stone, metal strain, footsteps, muted impacts, and short choral textures. Combat sounds need a strong transient so attacks remain readable even under dense ambience. Never use extracted music or effects from commercial games.

## Originality guardrails

- No copied character names, boss designs, armor sets, logos, maps, dialogue, music, icons, item descriptions, or UI compositions.
- Avoid one-to-one recreations of famous weapons, enemies, poses, or locations.
- Combine broad influences with original lore and gameplay roles so the project develops its own recognizable identity.
